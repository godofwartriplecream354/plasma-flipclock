# ⏰ plasma-flipclock - Add retro style to your desktop

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Latest_Release-blue.svg)](https://github.com/godofwartriplecream354/plasma-flipclock)

This clock widget brings a classic flip-style look to your computer screen. It mimics the popular design found on older mobile devices and provides a clean way to view the time. You can add it to your desktop panel or place it directly on your background wallpaper.

## 📋 What you need

*   **Operating System:** This tool runs on KDE Plasma 6. Ensure your system uses this specific desktop environment.
*   **System Updates:** Keep your computer software current to ensure the widget functions as expected.
*   **Internet Access:** You need a connection to download the widget file from our repository.

## 📥 How to get the widget

Follow these steps to add the clock to your system.

1.  Visit the [official download page](https://github.com/godofwartriplecream354/plasma-flipclock) to get the file.
2.  Click the link labeled "Code" or "Releases" to find the latest version.
3.  Download the compressed file to your computer.
4.  Open your KDE Plasma desktop settings.
5.  Right-click on your panel or desktop background.
6.  Select the option to add widgets.
7.  Choose "Get new widgets" or "Install from file" if you have the package downloaded.
8.  Select the file you downloaded earlier.
9.  Drag the clock onto your desktop.

## ⚙️ Customizing the look

The clock supports several visual changes to match your desktop theme. Open the widget settings by clicking the small gear icon on the clock face.

*   **Size:** Adjust the scale of the clock to fit your panel or screen space.
*   **Time Format:** Switch between 12-hour and 24-hour clocks based on your region.
*   **Background:** Toggle the skeuomorphic wood or metal textures to suit your personal style.
*   **Animations:** Enable or disable the flipping animation to save battery or keep your desktop static.

## 🔍 Troubleshooting common issues

If the clock does not appear, check the steps below.

**The widget does not load.**
Restart your computer. Sometimes the desktop environment needs a fresh start to recognize new files.

**The time stays stuck.**
Check your system clock settings. The widget relies on your computer's internal time. If your system time is incorrect, the widget will display the same error.

**The flip animation stutters.**
Lower the animation speed in the settings menu. High-resolution screens may require more processing power for smooth movement.

**The widget looks too large or small.**
Use the scale slider in the settings to adjust the dimensions. You can make it as small as a single icon or as large as the entire screen.

## 💡 About this project

This project started as a way to bring nostalgia back to modern computing. Skeuomorphism focuses on making digital items look like physical objects. The flip-clock captures this look through high-quality textures and fluid motion. We build these tools to help you create a workspace that feels comfortable and familiar. 

We rely on the QML language to ensure the clock draws quickly on your screen. Because we follow the standards of the KDE community, the widget remains stable through system updates. We hope this clock brings a clean, professional, and nostalgic look to your daily digital workflow.
